I'm writing this post with a big delay. On the past 11th of February I summited Toubkal. It was quite an easy summit, all in all; the major difficulties were logistic and, let's say, not mountain-related.
It was a pretty fun solo summit, but if you enjoy mountains, you might prefer to take your tastes elsewhere.

## Preparations

The winter of 2026 was very rigorous. We had [record levels of precipitation](https://climate.copernicus.eu/exceptional-rainfall-and-flooding-parts-europe-and-north-africa-february) and very unstable weather. For example, ice climbing was viable for the first time in 5 years in Portugal!
I had a long-time idea to solo Monte Perdido: not too technical, no glaciar dangers and juicy enough that cold set my head and test it to longer and higher flights.
